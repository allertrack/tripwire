Tighten-only, two-key relax, fail-safe

  
Automation can only make a market safer.

  

    
Tighten-only
Reports and guardians raise the level, never lower it. A compromised watcher can halt borrowing, not unfreeze anything.

    
Two keys to relax
Governance proposes and waits out the delay. Execution needs no new trip since, plus a fresh watcher observation that agrees.

    
Fails safe
If the watcher misses its heartbeat, the guard is at least Caution, with no transaction needed.

  






  
Recovery · the watcher agrees, anyone executes the matured relax
